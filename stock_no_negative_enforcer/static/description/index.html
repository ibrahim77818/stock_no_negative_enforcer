Strict Negative Stock Prevention

Never sell or ship what is not on the shelf. A negative-stock switch on the warehouse, the location, the product category and the product decides, for every product and location, whether stock may go below zero. Orders are checked when confirmed, every stock move is checked when it is done, and the check runs under a database row lock.

Why this module

In Odoo 17, stock can silently go below zero whenever a transfer is validated with more quantity than is on hand. That is convenient for some products and a data-quality problem for others. You need to decide product by product and place by place, and you need a clear message at the moment of sale rather than a negative quant discovered at stock-take.

In standard Odoo 17

  • A sale order can be confirmed for goods that are not in stock, and nothing tells the salesperson.
  • Validating a delivery with a picked quantity above the on-hand quantity creates a negative quant without a warning.
  • There is no setting to allow negative stock for some warehouses, locations, categories or products and forbid it for the others.
  • Two users validating at the same time on the same stock have no shared lock beyond what the ORM happens to give them.
  • When a shortage does surface, the error is generic and does not say which product, which place or how much is missing.

With this module

  • An Allow Negative Stock switch on Warehouse, Location, Product Category and Product, off by default everywhere.
  • A clear rule: negative stock is possible only when every applicable level is On. A level that is Off always wins over the ones above it.
  • Sale orders are checked at confirmation: demand is totalled per product and compared with the free stock of the order’s warehouse.
  • Every move out of an internal location is checked when it is done, so deliveries, immediate transfers and manual moves cannot create negative stock either.
  • The stock being consumed is locked with SELECT … FOR UPDATE before it is checked, so concurrent users cannot both take the last unit.
  • A user-friendly error names the product, the place, the requested, available and missing quantities and the levels that block it.

How it works

1
Configure
Switch Allow Negative Stock on only where overselling is acceptable. Everything else stays strict.
2
Sell
Confirm a quotation. The order is checked against the free stock of its warehouse before anything is created.
3
Move
When a transfer is done, each quantity leaving an internal location is checked under a row lock.
4
Learn why
A blocked user sees what is missing and which level (product, category, location, warehouse) forbids negative stock.

Features

The screenshots follow one demo scenario: a US-dollar company with two warehouses, Main Warehouse (WH) and Outlet Store (OUT). Main Warehouse holds 8 Standing Desks, 20 Executive Chairs and 14 Whiteboards; the Outlet holds 4 Executive Chairs and 3 Whiteboards and no desks. The Pre-order Speaker has no stock and Allow Negative Stock switched on at every level.

Feature 1

Switch on the warehouse

Open Inventory › Configuration › Warehouses. Allow Negative Stock is a toggle under the address and applies to every location that belongs to the warehouse. It is off by default.

Feature 2

Switch on the location

The same switch on Inventory › Configuration › Locations lets you open a single place, for example a staging or pre-order shelf, while the rest of the warehouse stays strict. It is shown for internal locations only.

Feature 3

Switch on the product category

On the Product Category form the switch covers every product of the category. Use it for families that are routinely sold ahead of receipt.

Feature 4

Switch on the product

The product form has the final say. It is shown for storable products, next to the product category. Here the Whiteboard keeps it off even though its category allows negative stock.

Feature 5

Any level can say no

The lowest Off level wins over every On level above it, and the error message lists exactly which levels are Off. The result for each product and location is cached and refreshed automatically when a switch, a product category or a location’s parent changes, so the lookup costs almost nothing on large databases.

A switch cannot be “unset”, so an Off level always blocks, whatever the levels above it say.
Feature 6

Sale orders are checked at confirmation

Confirming a quotation totals the demand per product over all its lines (in the product unit of measure) and compares it with the free stock (on hand minus reserved) of the order’s warehouse. If anything is short, nothing is confirmed and the message lists every short product with its requested, available and missing quantity. The Executive Chair line is fine, so it is not listed.

Feature 7

Every stock move is guarded

Orders are not the only way stock leaves. When a transfer is validated, each quantity leaving an internal location is checked as it is booked. The delivery below has 8 desks reserved and a picked quantity of 10, so the extra 2 would create negative stock and the validation is refused with the exact shortfall. The same guard covers immediate transfers, manual moves and any other module that books stock.

Feature 8

Each warehouse stands alone

Stock in one warehouse never covers a sale from another. Standing Desks exist only in Main Warehouse, so an order for the Outlet Store is refused even though 8 desks are available company-wide. Free stock is counted over the internal locations of the order’s warehouse only, and the warehouse switch applies only to its own locations.

Feature 9

Allowed means allowed

When every level is On, nothing is checked and nothing is blocked: the order is confirmed, the delivery is created and stock may go negative on delivery, exactly as in standard Odoo. This order for the pre-order item is confirmed with no stock on hand.

Feature 10

Safe under concurrent use

Locks are taken in a fixed order (by record id), which keeps deadlocks rare; PostgreSQL still detects and aborts a real deadlock, and Odoo retries the request. The automated test starts eight real database connections at once, each trying to ship 3 units out of 10, and asserts that exactly three succeed, five are blocked and the stock ends at 1, never below zero.

What happens when several users act on the same stock at the same time:
StepWhat the module does
1. LockRuns SELECT id FROM stock_quant … ORDER BY id FOR UPDATE on the quants of the product and location (or warehouse, for sale orders). Other transactions wait.
2. CheckReads the quantity and compares it with the request. Below zero with negative stock disabled: a clear UserError, nothing is written.
3. BookBooks the move. The lock is held until commit, so the next user sees the new quantity.
4. RetryIf a locked row was changed by another transaction, PostgreSQL raises a serialization failure and Odoo retries the request automatically. The retried request then sees the real quantity.

Where it applies

The guard only looks at moves that take storable products out of an internal location.

Checked a quantity leaves an internal location: delivery, internal transfer, manual or immediate transfer, scrap, any other module
Never blocked goods coming in: receipts, returns from customers, stock increases
Out of scope services and consumables, and non-internal locations (customers, vendors, inventory loss, transit)
Skipped at order level order lines with an explicit route (for example dropship or MTO) and down-payment lines; the move guard still protects the stock itself

Built for real-world corner cases

Multi-warehouse
The check uses the order’s warehouse and the internal locations beneath it. Other warehouses are never counted.
Multi-company
Only locations of the order’s company (or shared locations) count. Configuration is read with elevated rights, so users without stock access get the right message.
Several lines, one product
Lines of the same product are added up before the check, so two lines of 3 against 5 in stock are refused.
Units of measure
Demand is converted to the product’s unit of measure before it is compared with stock.
Reservations
Free stock is on hand minus reserved, so goods already promised to another order are not sold twice.
Lots and packages
The move guard is strict: it checks the quant for the exact product, location, lot, package and owner being moved.
Exact consumption
Taking exactly what is on hand is allowed. Only a result below zero is refused.
Kits
Not covered: kit components are not unpacked at order level. Their component moves are still caught by the move guard.
